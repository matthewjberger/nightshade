# Emission

Emission is light a surface gives off rather than color it reflects. It is not
bounded by one, so a material can put out more light than any lamp in the
scene, and what reaches the picture is decided afterwards by the exposure.

Bloom is not what makes something bright. It is what happens to light that is
already brighter than the picture can hold, so turning it off leaves the
brightness and takes away only the spill.
