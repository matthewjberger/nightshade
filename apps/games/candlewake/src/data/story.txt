-- Candlewake, written in the story format nightshade-story reads.

story Candlewake
opening The sea has gone quiet, which is worse than the noise.
opening You are on the shingle below the lighthouse, and the light is out.
start shore

value lit 0
value filled 0

room shore
  title The Shingle
  look Grey stones grind under you. The lighthouse stands above the tide line, and its door is open on the dark.
  way north hall

room hall
  title The Long Hall
  look Salt has eaten the plaster off the walls. A stair climbs into the dark, and a hatch lies open in the floor.
  way south shore
  way up stair when lit same 1 barred The stair is black above you. You will not climb it blind.
  way down cellar when lit same 1 barred The hatch is a hole full of night.

room stair
  title The Turning Stair
  look The steps turn and turn. Your light throws your shadow up the wall ahead of you.
  way down hall
  way up lamp

room lamp
  title The Lamp Room
  look Glass on every side, and the great lamp cold in the middle of it. Beyond the glass the sea waits for a light.
  way down stair

room cellar
  title The Cellar
  look Water stands ankle deep and takes the warmth out of you. Crates have swollen and split against the wall.
  way up hall

thing tinderbox
  words tinderbox tinder box flint
  look A tinderbox, dry inside, which is a small mercy.
  at shore
  portable
  taking The tinderbox is dry. You put it in your pocket.

thing door
  words door doorway
  look The door has stood open a long time. The wood has gone soft with salt.
  at shore

thing lantern
  words lantern brass
  look A brass lantern with a cold wick. It has been waiting for someone.
  at hall
  portable
  taking You lift the lantern. It is lighter than it should be.

thing oil
  words oil can tin
  look A tin of lamp oil, still heavy, still sealed.
  at cellar
  portable
  taking You take the tin. It sloshes.

thing beacon
  words beacon great wick
  look The beacon stands taller than you, with a wick as thick as your wrist and nothing in it to burn.
  at lamp

verb light lantern
  when holds lantern and holds tinderbox
  say You strike the tinderbox. The wick takes, and a small warm room moves with you wherever you go.
  set lit set 1
  barred You want the lantern in your hand and something to strike it with.

verb pour oil
  when holds oil and at lamp
  say You tip the tin into the reservoir, and the beacon drinks every drop of it.
  set filled set 1
  move oil gone
  barred There is nothing here worth pouring it into.

verb light beacon
  when filled same 1 and lit same 1
  say You touch the lantern to the wick. The beacon catches, and the whole room turns white.
  end Far out on the water a boat corrects its course. You sit down on the iron floor and wait for the morning.
  barred The wick is dry, and it will not take.

verb wait
  say You wait. The sea says nothing back.
