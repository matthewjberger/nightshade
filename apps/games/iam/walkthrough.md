# walkthrough

**This spoils the whole game.** Every password, every answer, every branch and
every ending is below. It is five short nights and it is meant to be worked
out. Read it after, or when you are stuck.

---

## how to work the machine

Read this bit even if you skip the rest. Most people who get stuck are stuck on
one of these.

**Logging in.** Type `guest` and press Enter. The password is on the sticky
note.

**Opening a program.** Click its icon on the desktop, or the start button at the
bottom left and pick it off the menu. Nothing is open when you sign in. Click
**iam** first.

**Talking to somebody.** Click a name in the left sidebar. The composer at the
bottom lists what you can say. Click a line to say it.

**Posting in a room.** Click a server in the sidebar, then a room under it. The
composer lists what you can post there. Each one can be posted once and then it
is gone.

**SHOW OFFLINE.** The `+ SHOW OFFLINE` line in the sidebar. People go on your
list before they come online, and you cannot see or open them until this is on.
**Turn it on now and leave it on.** tuck, wendy_h, op_9, the sysop, sundial,
newuser_40, nul and the archivist all hide behind it at some point, and the
archivist never comes out from behind it at all.

**PENDING.** Invitations land above the contacts. Click the row to accept. You
need this twice: the copy of you on friday, and `unknown` on saturday.

**Ending a night.** Start button, then **sign off**. It always talks to
iambot, whatever you have open, and says what the night is still waiting on.
Saying it to iambot yourself works too, from the composer, with his thread
open.

**Windows.** Drag a bar to carry a window. Double click a bar to fill the
desktop, and again to give it back. Drag the bar of one that is filled and it
comes off the top under your cursor. Filling the desktop leaves the bar along
the bottom alone.

**The note on the desktop.** Top right, in yellow. It has a square for each of
tonight's two jobs. A job you have not been told about yet reads `????`, so on
the first night the note is mostly question marks and fills in as you learn
what the night is even asking for. It says so in the corner when a square gets
its words, when one is ticked, and when the last one is.

**Sign off is dead until the night is finished.** It greys out in the start
menu and does nothing. What is missing is on the note.

**The start menu** holds the programs the machine has, and under a line, the
two ways out: **sign off**, and **switch users**, which takes you back to the
login screen to open somebody else's account.

**The browser.** Three ways to move:

1. Click a **link** in the **GO TO** strip, which runs across the page just
   under the address bar. That strip is how you get anywhere.
2. Click a **line in a table**. Board names, thread titles and the member list
   are all clickable.
3. Click the **address bar**, type an address, press Enter.

Bookmarks appear on the bar as you learn what they are for. The folder marked
`r's stuff` holds the ones the last occupant filed.

**Fields on a page.** Click the field, type, press Enter. The search, the
directory, the member list, rot13, the introductions template, the hire form
and the transfer all have one.

**The search.** `holloway.local/search`, on the bookmarks bar and the first
link on the intranet. It has no browse and no list of what it holds. It only
answers what you type at it, which makes it the one place in the game where
what you know is literally the only thing that opens anything. Come back to it
every time you learn a word.

**The magnifying glass.** Hold the **right mouse button**. A lens comes up over
the pointer and magnifies the tube itself. The forum tables and the case files
are the places you will want it.

**Notices.** Minimize or close the messenger and anything addressed to you
comes up in the bottom right corner, three at a time: lines somebody is typing
at you, and replies to what you posted in a room. What a room says on its own
schedule stays quiet, because it is not talking to you. Click a notice to open
the messenger on that thread or that room.

**The log.** Click the clock on the taskbar. Every notice this week is kept
there, newest first, with when it landed. Scroll it with the wheel. Nothing on
this machine throws anything away.

## the fastest way through

Thirteen things to do, and signing off at the end of each night. Nothing here
is optional and nothing else is needed. The rest of this document is the long
way round, which is the way it was meant to be played.

**Night done** below means the note in the corner has no squares left, and you
can go start → **sign off**. Do that at the end of every one of them.

Two things make it short. Almost everything a night asks for is on a page, and a
page is reached by clicking the address bar and typing. And the login screen
checks the password and nothing else, so you can open r.mears with `8815` on the
first night without having worked out what 8815 is.

building"**. d.okonkwo wrote down the office from memory and posted it beside

Two of them only open in an order.

   says two of them exist. Once you have read it, the stairwell offers you the

   have thought to try.

   it the other way round and the chairs are just chairs. Do it in this order
   and the author tells you why he stopped moving the ninth one.

The corridor is not the end of it. There is a way past the point where the
skirting board stops, and it does not open until saturday, and it does not
open at all until you have read the file with your handle on it. It is under
**the seam**, below.

not the register the rest of it is written in. The last one is at the doors: he
could not remember the street, he had been trying for two years, and he can
remember the doors from the inside and from the outside and not standing in
between.
